Here is a cleaned-up current shortlist of 15 inexpensive hotels that are presently sold as 5-star hotels by major booking/meta sources, with my view on whether each is only “booking-site 5-star” or also a genuinely appealing luxury bargain. Prices below are live indicative prices I found now for near-term dates, so they are useful for ranking/value sense, not a guaranteed quote for your exact future nights. (expedia)
Best current sub-€100 / sub-US$110 shortlist
1. Grand Ion Delemen Hotel, Genting Highlands — about US$27–36. It is explicitly classified as 5-star on Traveloka and shows rates from US$27 on KAYAK. My take: extremely cheap on paper, but more “cheap 5-star inventory” than dream luxury stay. (Traveloka)
2. The Royal Surakarta Heritage – Handwritten Collection, Solo — about £27 / US$33. Expedia shows £27 including taxes and fees, and KAYAK shows rates from US$33. My take: one of the best-value entries because it is cheap and has more character than a generic business hotel. (expedia)
3. NH Collection Bogotá WTC Royal, Bogotá — about €31–45 / US$36–49. KAYAK shows multiple deals in that range. My take: definitely a market-accepted 5-star, but more polished business hotel than aspirational luxury. (KAYAK)
4. Boton Blue Hotel & Spa, Nha Trang — about US$40–59. KAYAK shows rates from US$40, while Expedia shows prices from US$59 including taxes and fees. My take: strong resort-style value, though not top-tier luxury. (KAYAK)
5. Grand Mercure Medan Angkasa, Medan — about US$46 / ฿1,277 total. Accor calls it a 5-star hotel, Agoda cites an average around US$46, and Expedia Thailand showed ฿1,277 current price. My take: very good price, but quality looks more upper-upscale than truly special. (all.accor.com)
6. Hotel Ickale, Ankara — about US$48 / £70 / €75 average. Booking describes it as a 5-star experience; KAYAK shows rates from US$48, and Agoda says the past-month average was about €75. My take: legitimate booking-site 5-star, classic business/wellness hotel, not elite luxury. (Booking.com)
7. The Phoenix Hotel Yogyakarta – Handwritten Collection — about US$61 / roughly ¥7,385 before taxes. Expedia lists it as a 5-star hotel and shows a current starting price around ¥7,385; Expedia’s luxury-hotel page also labels it 5-star. My take: one of the strongest and most attractive picks on the whole list. (expedia)
8. Novotel Ahmedabad — about US$56–70. Booking calls it a 5-star contemporary business hotel, and KAYAK shows current rates in the US$56–70 range. My take: sensible, dependable, good-value 5-star in the Indian business-hotel sense. (Booking.com)
9. Pleiada Boutique Hotel & Spa, Iași — about €61 / US$81. Booking explicitly calls it 5-star, KAYAK IE shows rates from €61, and KAYAK US shows rates from US$81. My take: one of the more convincing boutique-luxury bargains here. (Booking.com)
10. I’M Hotel, Makati — about US$70–105. Orbitz describes it as a 5-star hotel, KAYAK says recent bookings were as low as US$70, and Expedia currently shows US$97–105 including taxes and fees for near-term dates. My take: a very solid urban luxury-value option. (orbitz)
11. Meliá Yangon — about US$60–73. Meliá positions it as its flagship Yangon luxury property, and KAYAK shows recent bookings around US$73, with rates from US$60. My take: credible inexpensive luxury, though market conditions in Myanmar can distort pricing. (Melia Hotels)
12. Hotel Majapahit Surabaya – MGallery — about £77. Booking and KAYAK present it as 5-star, and Expedia shows starting prices around £77 including taxes and fees. My take: probably the single most appealing “real experience” hotel in this shortlist under the price ceiling. (Booking.com)
13. Graffit Gallery Design Hotel, Varna — about US$72–82. KAYAK shows current deals starting around US$72 and going into the US$80s. My take: stylish and well-priced, but more design-forward upscale than classic grand luxury. (KAYAK)
14. Real Marina Hotel & Spa, Olhão — about US$74, though sometimes much higher. Booking presents it as 5-star, and KAYAK shows rates from US$74. My take: strong value when it drops, but less reliably cheap than the Asian picks. (Booking.com)
15. Malak Regency Hotel, Sarajevo — about £74–82, with some sources showing best deals from US$70 and average around €91.95 recently. My take: a respectable regional 5-star value, though not a bucket-list luxury hotel. (expedia)
My top 5 from this list if the goal is “cheap but still feels special”
I would prioritize Hotel Majapahit Surabaya, The Phoenix Yogyakarta, The Royal Surakarta Heritage, Pleiada Boutique Hotel & Spa, and I’M Hotel Makati. Those five look like the best combination of low price and a stay that might actually feel memorable rather than merely “technically 5-star.” (expedia)
My caution list
The ones I would treat most cautiously are Grand Ion Delemen, Hotel Ickale, NH Collection Bogotá WTC Royal, and Grand Mercure Medan Angkasa. They are still valid for this kind of list, but they read to me more like “good deal on a full-service upscale hotel” than “luxury treat.” (Traveloka)
The old notes were basically correct on the big point: yes, sub-€100 5-star hotels absolutely still exist, especially in Indonesia, India, Southeast Asia, parts of Eastern Europe, and a few softer-demand city markets. The catch is that “5-star” is often a market/category label, not a guarantee of Four Seasons-level luxury. (Booking.com)
