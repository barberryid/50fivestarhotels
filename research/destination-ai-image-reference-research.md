# Destination AI Image Reference Research

Online pages were reviewed only as visual references. No downloaded or source photos are used as website assets. The final destination-card files are newly AI-generated editorial images.

## Siem Reap - Angkor Wat at sunrise

### Reference URLs reviewed
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/668/
- https://www.tourismcambodia.com/attractions/angkor/angkor-wat.htm
- https://www.apsaraauthority.gov.kh/

### Observed visual features
- Architecture / landscape: Khmer temple towers, long horizontal causeway forms, lotus pond reflection.
- Colours: muted stone, dark temple silhouette, pink-gold dawn sky, reflective water.
- Light: sunrise backlight with gentle haze and low contrast.
- Composition: wide symmetrical temple view with water foreground.
- Important details to preserve: five-tower silhouette, lotus pond, calm spiritual scale.
- Details to avoid: fantasy towers, crowds in the foreground, exaggerated orange HDR skies.

### Recommended AI image direction
- Main prompt direction: Angkor Wat silhouette reflected in a lotus pond at restrained pink-gold sunrise.
- Alternative prompt direction: lower, more intimate pond-edge view with temple towers still clear.

### Manual review notes
- Confirm temple silhouette reads as Angkor Wat rather than generic Khmer fantasy architecture.
