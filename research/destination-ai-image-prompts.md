# Destination AI Image Prompts

Base style for all images:

Create a realistic editorial travel photograph for an affordable-luxury travel website. Landscape composition, 16:10 aspect ratio, natural light, premium but believable colour grading, sharp but not overprocessed, no text, no logos, no watermark, no fake signage, no surreal architecture. The image should work as a destination card thumbnail, with a clear subject, elegant foreground/midground/background layering, and enough visual calm for card cropping.

Shared negative prompt / avoid list:

No collage, no split screen, no fantasy architecture, no cartoon style, no illustration, no exaggerated neon colours, no warped columns, no distorted domes, no impossible windows, no readable text, no fake brand logo, no crowds, no people dominating the frame, no low-resolution look, no fisheye distortion, no over-saturated HDR, no unrealistic reflections, no direct copy of any reference photo.
