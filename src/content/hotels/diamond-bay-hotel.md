## Why it is interesting

Diamond Bay Hotel is not the most distinctive under-€50 candidate, but it is useful because Nha Trang can support several credible low-rate hotel options. It brings a mass-market resort-city angle: pool, sea views, beach access and a broader leisure setting.

## Why it is good value

At visible rates from around €40, a hotel with resort-city facilities can be good value if the room category is acceptable. The editorial case depends less on charm and more on what the price buys in a beach destination.

## Price notes

Compare room categories carefully. A low entry-level room and a sea-view room may produce very different experiences. Check whether breakfast, taxes and cancellation are included.

## Location and nearby sights

Nha Trang Beach, the bay, island trips, Po Nagar Cham Towers and Hon Chong are the core reasons to consider the city. Diamond Bay works best as part of a low-cost coastal itinerary rather than as a destination hotel in itself.

## Food and things to do nearby

The local seafood and cafe scene is strong enough to carry a budget-conscious trip. Bun cha ca, nem nuong and market snacks keep daily costs low while the hotel adds a sense of comfort.

## Best time to book

Search several dates and compare against Havana, Virgo and TTC Hotel Michelia. If several hotels are under €50 at once, choose the one with the strongest recent reviews and best room category.

## Watch out for

The hotel is less characterful than the Central Java heritage picks. It should be framed as practical beach value, not emotional luxury.

## Verdict

Good value. Diamond Bay Hotel is a credible Nha Trang under-€50 candidate, but mainly as a backup or comparison point rather than the first profile to develop.
