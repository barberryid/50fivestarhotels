## Why it is interesting

Opened in 1893, the Pfister is a Victorian grand hotel housing the largest collection of 19th-century European art in any US hotel. NBA visiting teams have stayed here for decades. A complete renovation was finished in early 2025. The rooftop pool on the 23rd floor looks over Lake Michigan, and the Michelin Guide lists it among the country's notable hotel stays - at rates that would be implausible in Chicago, three hours to the south.

## Why it is good value

The Pfister has a 9.5 Booking.com score from over 2,500 guests - the most data-confident luxury property on this list - and costs less per night than a standard room at a Chicago convention hotel.

## Price notes

USD $136-$240/night shoulder season; three-phase renovation completed early 2025.

## Location and nearby sights

Milwaukee Art Museum (Calatrava wing) - 10 minutes on foot; the brise-soleil "wings" that open at noon are one of the most photographed architectural moments in the US Midwest.

Milwaukee Public Market and Third Ward - the city's artisan food hall and independent restaurant district, walkable from the hotel, with excellent Midwestern craft brewing.

Harley-Davidson Museum - 15 minutes by cab; an unexpectedly compelling museum with superb exhibition design and some of the most photogenic motorcycles in the world.

## Food and things to do nearby

Milwaukee Public Market and the Third Ward are walkable from the hotel, with independent restaurants and excellent Midwestern craft brewing.

## Best time to book

Check shoulder-season dates in the USD $136-$240/night range.
