## Why it is interesting

Olhão is the working fishing town that the Algarve's resort infrastructure largely bypassed — and that is precisely its appeal. The daily market, the tiled cubist buildings, the ferries to the barrier islands of the Ria Formosa Natural Park and the fish restaurants around the harbour make it one of southern Portugal's most authentic and rewarding towns. The Real Marina Hotel & Spa offers a comfortable, spa-equipped base here with marina views.

## Why it is good value

Portugal remains relatively affordable by Western European standards, and Olhão sits at a price point below the polished resort towns of Vilamoura and Vale do Lobo while offering a more characterful experience. A marina spa hotel here represents good value in the context of European coastal travel.

## Price notes

The Algarve's high season runs June to September, with July and August commanding premium rates. May and October offer excellent weather with more competitive pricing. The winter months are very quiet and rates drop significantly, though some facilities may have reduced hours.

## Location and nearby sights

The hotel's marina position gives direct views over the Ria Formosa lagoon system. Ferry services to the barrier islands — Ilha da Culatra, Ilha do Armona — run from the town dock. Tavira (one of the Algarve's most beautiful towns) is 15 minutes east by train.

## Food and things to do nearby

Olhão is widely regarded as the Algarve's best town for fresh seafood. The municipal market (open mornings, closed Sunday) is one of Portugal's finest covered food markets. Arroz de lingueirão (razor clam rice), grilled sea bream, perceves (barnacles) and cataplana (shellfish stew) are the essential eating. The hotel's own dining is reliable and well reviewed.

## Best time to book

May–June and September–October offer the best combination of weather, beach conditions, market activity and pricing. July–August is peak and busy; November–March is very quiet.

## Watch out for

Olhão is genuinely a working town rather than a polished resort. The immediate surroundings of the hotel are pleasant, but the broader town has rough edges that some travellers will find charming and others will find underwhelming compared with more manicured Algarve alternatives. The main Atlantic beaches require a ferry or car.

## Verdict

Good value. A well-positioned spa hotel in the Algarve's most authentic and food-focused town, best suited to travellers who want character and seafood over polished resort infrastructure.
