## Why it is interesting

Hotel Majapahit is the kind of hotel that makes the idea of affordable five-star travel feel real. Built in 1910 and carrying genuine colonial history — it was the site of the Surabaya flag incident of 1945 — it has atmosphere, character and a sense of place that most budget hotels could never replicate. The MGallery Collection stamp adds an international quality layer without erasing the local heritage feel.

## Why it is good value

This is not just a cheap business hotel with a five-star label. It is a characterful heritage stay that can often appear at prices far below what similar historic hotels would cost in London, Amsterdam or Singapore. For travellers who care about place and history as much as facilities, the value case is compelling.

## Price notes

Rates vary by date, room type, tax treatment and cancellation policy. It is usually worth checking midweek and outside major Indonesian holiday periods. Prices are generally more competitive outside the July–August peak and major Lebaran travel windows.

## Location and nearby sights

Surabaya is Indonesia's second-largest city and one of its most underrated. Use the hotel as a base for the old town colonial district, the House of Sampoerna (Indonesia's most compelling tobacco heritage museum), and the Heroes Monument. The Arab Quarter and Chinatown are within easy reach.

## Food and things to do nearby

Surabaya is an outstanding city for East Java food. Rawon — the dark beef soup made with black kluwek nuts — is the dish to prioritise. Sate, rujak cingur and Surabaya's lively night markets round out a compelling food itinerary. The hotel's own dining is solid, but the streets around it are equally rewarding.

## Best time to book

Best checked outside Indonesian holiday peaks and major domestic travel periods. The shoulder months of April–May and September–October often offer better availability and rates.

## Watch out for

Expect heritage character rather than flawless new-build luxury. Some travellers may find the style more classic than contemporary, and the city of Surabaya is not as immediately charming as Yogyakarta or Bali for first-time visitors. It rewards travellers who engage with it seriously.

## Verdict

Exceptional value. This is one of the strongest launch hotels because the price, history and destination angle work together into a genuinely compelling travel idea.
