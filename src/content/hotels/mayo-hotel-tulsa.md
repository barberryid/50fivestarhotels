## Why it is interesting

Built in 1925 and restored to its original Art Deco splendour, the Mayo sits at the epicentre of Tulsa's extraordinary downtown - the most architecturally intact Art Deco city centre in the United States. The Penthouse Rooftop Lounge and the Boiler Room restaurant anchor the hotel's social life, while the surrounding blocks hold one of America's best-kept heritage walking circuits.
