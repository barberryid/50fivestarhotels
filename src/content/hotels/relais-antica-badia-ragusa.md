## Why it is interesting

Relais Antica Badia is the Italy discovery pick: a small historic five-star in Ragusa, one of the best bases for the Val di Noto baroque circuit. It is not the obvious Sicily luxury answer, and that is exactly why it belongs here.

## Why it is good value

The pricing gap between Ragusa and Sicily's famous coastal luxury destinations can be dramatic. Travellers willing to choose baroque streets, churches, food and road-trip exploring over beach-club glamour can find a much more affordable five-star stay.

## Price notes

The realistic shoulder-season range is about €120-€190, with higher rates possible on peak Sicily dates. Spring, October, November and non-holiday weekdays are the cleanest value windows.

## Location and nearby sights

The hotel is a useful base for Ragusa Superiore, Ragusa Ibla and the wider Val di Noto circuit. Modica, Scicli and Noto make the stay feel bigger than a single small-city hotel break.

## Food and things to do nearby

This is a strong food-and-architecture pick. Modica chocolate, Sicilian pastries, ricotta, Nero d'Avola and baroque-town trattorias give the stay a specific sense of place.

## Best time to book

Book away from peak summer and major holiday periods. Autumn is especially appealing because the weather, food and driving conditions all work in the hotel's favour.

## Watch out for

The hotel is more characterful than polished, and Ragusa is easiest with a car. Readers expecting a large resort with broad facilities may miss the point.

## Verdict

Exceptional value. Relais Antica Badia turns a lesser-known Sicilian city into one of Italy's most compelling affordable five-star stories.
