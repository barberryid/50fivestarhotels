## Why it is interesting

Virgo Hotel is included because the price evidence is almost absurdly low for a five-star-labelled property. It is not the most atmospheric hotel in the collection, and it should not be confused with a polished resort. Its value is as a research marker: Nha Trang can produce hotel rates that are difficult to believe until checked.

## Why it is good value

At visible snapshot rates from around €18, the value question changes. The hotel does not need to deliver international luxury to be interesting; it needs to be clean, credible, well located and significantly better than the price suggests.

## Price notes

Ultra-low prices require extra caution. Check whether the rate is prepaid, non-refundable, tax-exclusive, room-category specific or tied to a weak date. Also read recent reviews, not only the headline score.

## Location and nearby sights

The broader Nha Trang setting gives the hotel its usefulness: beach access, Cham towers, island trips, cafes and seafood. It is best treated as a low-cost city-beach base.

## Food and things to do nearby

Nha Trang is easy for seafood, fish-noodle soup, grilled nem nuong and casual Vietnamese snacks. The food-and-beach combination helps make a cheap hotel stay feel like a real trip rather than a compromise.

## Best time to book

Look outside domestic holiday peaks and compare midweek dates. If a rate looks unusually low, check whether better room categories remain affordable.

## Watch out for

The editorial ceiling is lower than Havana Nha Trang Hotel. Virgo may be a good-value stay, but it is less distinctive and less emotionally persuasive. Use it as backup unless the core article is about the cheapest credible five-star-labelled hotels found worldwide.

## Verdict

Good value. Virgo Hotel is credible enough to list as an under-€50 candidate, but it needs review and image checks before becoming a priority profile.
