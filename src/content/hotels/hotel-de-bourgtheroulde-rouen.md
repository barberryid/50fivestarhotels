## Why it is interesting

Hotel de Bourgtheroulde gives Rouen a hotel with real architectural authority: a Gothic-Renaissance mansion in a city already rich with cathedral drama, Joan of Arc history and half-timbered streets. It is exactly the sort of place travellers may walk past without realizing it is a hotel.

## Why it is good value

Rouen is a better destination than its hotel pricing often implies. The city has Monet, medieval streets, Normandy food and a major cathedral, but it is not priced like Paris or the most obvious French leisure markets. That gap lets a five-star heritage hotel appear below €250 on the right dates.

## Price notes

Shoulder-season rates are the target. Normandy event periods, D-Day anniversary travel and high-demand weekends can push prices out of range. Verify the final rate including taxes, room type and cancellation terms.

## Location and nearby sights

The hotel is central enough for Rouen Cathedral, Place du Vieux Marche and the Gros Horloge on foot. It works well for a car-free Normandy city break or as a more atmospheric base before moving on to the coast.

## Food and things to do nearby

Rouen is a strong food city if the reader likes Normandy cooking: cider, Calvados, cheese, sole a la normande and traditional brasseries. The cathedral and old town provide the main visual structure, while museums and churches fill out a two-night stay.

## Best time to book

Spring and autumn are the safest value windows. Early summer can be attractive, but check Normandy event calendars and D-Day anniversary demand.
