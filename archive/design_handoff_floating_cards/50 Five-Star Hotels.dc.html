How we choose

One test, honestly applied.

Every candidate must make a seasoned traveller think: I didn't know I could have that kind of stay at that price. We weigh the hotel and the destination equally, reject 60–70% of what we look at, and never write aspirational filler. Drawbacks are stated plainly.
